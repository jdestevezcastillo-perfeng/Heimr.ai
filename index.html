How It Works

Three steps from raw results to actionable insights.

01

Analyze Your Results

Point Heimr at any load test result file. Auto-detects JMeter, k6, Gatling, or Locust formats.

$ heimr analyze results.jtl
02

Connect Observability

Optionally connect Prometheus, Loki, and Tempo for multi-signal correlation during the test window.

$ heimr analyze results.jtl --prometheus-url http://localhost:9090
03

Get AI Explanation

Receive a comprehensive report with root cause analysis, evidence citations, and specific recommendations.

$ heimr analyze results.jtl --explain --output report.md
